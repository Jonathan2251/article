解決方案
~~~~~~~~

問題在於與英文老師練習口說的費用非常高。我對這項業務的願景之
一，就是要讓英文學習「**平民化 democratize English**」。

我目前只想到中學生的方案。

成人或高中生都可以像我一樣自學英文。AI 和 YouTube 提供大量免
費的口說與寫作素材。當下一代的英文程度提升後，他們也能彼此練
習，與自己的英文夥伴對話。畢竟，人類比起機器，更喜歡與人類交
談，尤其是青少年與女性。

1. Copilot AI 提供無限時間的聊天，並能以文字或語音回答任何英
   文問題。

2. 跟讀（`Shadowing <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0l8pQde4Wo>`_）：

   **不要要求學生錄音並回放自己的聲音。  
   大多數人都不喜歡聽到自己被錄下來的聲音，即使是母語也一樣。**

   根據我與你一起練習口說的經驗，我曾詢問：跟讀（例如跟著
   YouTube 英文故事影片重複）是否是適合我的英文學習方法。

   是的，Jonathan —— 根據你的練習方式，跟讀確實是最適合你的
   方法之一。

   簡短回答:

   跟讀對你非常有效，因為它訓練的正是你想提升的能力：發音、
   節奏、流利度、自信，以及自然的語音模式。

   為什麼跟讀特別適合你:

   以下是我從你的英文練習中觀察到的：

   * 你已能理解英文內容（Podcast、影片、故事）。
   * 你的主要挑戰是口說流利度，而不是單字或文法。
   * 你想要聽起來自然，而不是「教科書英文」。
   * 你最擅長透過聽、重複、感受語調節奏來學習。
   * 你有動力且持續練習 —— 非常適合跟讀。

   跟讀正好能強化這些優勢。

   跟讀實際訓練的能力:

   * 發音 —— 模仿口型與聲音
   * 語調 —— 像母語者一樣的升降語氣
   * 節奏 —— 重音與語速的掌握
   * 流利度 —— 不停頓地說話
   * 聽力精準度 —— 捕捉平常容易忽略的細節

   這些正是你想提升的能力。

   為什麼比「只聊天」更有效:

   與我聊天能提升你的英文思考能力；跟讀則訓練你的口腔與耳朵。

   兩者都需要。

   跟讀能建立口說的肌肉記憶 —— 這是單靠對話無法做到的。

Solution
~~~~~~~~

The problem is that talking with English teachers is expensive. One of my
visions for this business is to **democratize English**.

Adult learners or high school students can learn English by themselves, just
as I did. AI and YouTube provide free materials for speaking and writing.
When the next generation reaches a higher English level, they can also learn
from each other by talking with their English partners. After all, humans
prefer talking with humans more than with machines, especially teenagers and
women.

1. Copilot AI provides unlimited time for chatting and answers any English
   question in text or voice.

2. `Shadowing <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0l8pQde4Wo>`_:

   Do not ask students to record and listen to their own speech. 
   Most people dislike hearing their own recorded voice, even in their
   native language. 

   According to the English speaking practice I have done with you, I asked
   whether shadowing (repeating after a YouTube English story video) is a
   good method for me.

   Yes, Jonathan — shadowing is one of the best methods for you, based on
   how you practice English with me.

   Short answer:

   Shadowing is very effective for you because it trains the exact skills you
   want to improve: pronunciation, rhythm, fluency, confidence, and natural
   speaking patterns.

   Why shadowing works especially well for you;

   Here is what I have noticed from your English practice:

   * You already understand English content (podcasts, videos, stories).
   * Your main challenge is speaking fluently, not vocabulary or grammar.
   * You want to sound natural, not like “textbook English.”
   * You learn best by listening, repeating, and feeling the rhythm.
   * You are motivated and consistent — perfect for shadowing.

   Shadowing directly targets these strengths.

   What shadowing actually trains:

   * Pronunciation — copying mouth shapes and sounds
   * Intonation — rising and falling tone like native speakers
   * Rhythm — stress patterns and pacing
   * Fluency — speaking without stopping to think
   * Listening accuracy — catching details you normally miss

   These are exactly the skills you want to improve.

   Why it works better than “just talking”:

   Talking with me helps your thinking in English. Shadowing helps your
   mouth and ears in English.

   You need both.

   Shadowing builds the muscle memory of speaking — something conversation
   alone cannot do.
